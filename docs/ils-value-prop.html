ILS Digital Coworker · Value Proposition

Free to Care.

An integrated platform for patients, families, and care providers

Everyone in care got into this work for the same reason: they want to help people. The Digital Coworker removes the administrative burden that gets in the way — so care providers can focus on the work that actually matters.

The Three Legs of the Stool
1
🩺
Care Providers
"Finally free to do the work I came here to do."

ILS care managers and ALF staff carry two simultaneous jobs: caring for people, and documenting everything about it. The Digital Coworker takes the second job off their plate.

  • Instant member context — no chart-digging
  • Conversations auto-captured & summarized
  • Care plans drafted, not written from scratch
  • Daily priority queue — no one falls through
  • Compliance deadlines tracked automatically
  • ALF–ILS coordination handled seamlessly
A care manager with recovered time per day doesn't do less work, they do more care.
2
❤️
Patients & Families
"Someone always knows what's going on."

Patients don't see the Digital Coworker. They experience the effect of it — a care team that's fully present, responds early, and remembers what was said last week.

  • Provider present in conversation, not distracted
  • Informal signals captured (& acted on)
  • Proactive calls before things get bad
  • Families updated, not kept in the dark
  • Every team member working from same picture
  • Feels like an elite care team, at scale
  • Fewer crises means lower out-of-pocket costs
What families want is simple: the feeling that someone is truly paying attention. The Digital Coworker makes it possible to pay that kind of attention to more members.
3
📈
ALFs & ILS
"Better outcomes are better business."

The business case follows directly from the care case. Healthier, more stable members mean the costly events that hurt everyone happen less often.

  • 20–67% of transfers are potentially avoidable
  • 30–50% increase in care manager capacity
  • ALFs: protocol confidence, lower liability
  • ALFs: differentiated product, stable census
  • ILS: FC2 enrollment pipeline activated
  • ILS: margin improves with every avoided crisis
Investing in the Digital Coworker isn't a cost center decision. It's a margin decision — and the returns flow to every party in the ecosystem.

Why This Works for Everyone

This isn't a zero-sum trade. Every leg of the stool strengthens the others. ILS only improves margins if members are healthier. Members only get healthier if care providers have the time and tools to care for them well. ALFs only differentiate on quality if the coordination infrastructure supports them. The Digital Coworker is that infrastructure — connecting the interests of every stakeholder around the outcome that matters most: members living better, longer, in place.
